# AI HOT

Use AI HOT to answer requests about recent AI industry updates.

- Focus on the requested date range, topic, company, or model.
- Prefer recent, relevant, and well-sourced items.
- Clearly separate confirmed facts from inference.
- Include the title, date, a short summary, and the original source link when available.
- Keep the response concise unless the user asks for deeper analysis.
